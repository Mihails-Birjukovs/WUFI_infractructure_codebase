# REF/HEMP Campaign 2 — preparation status and scientific gates (2026-11-14)

**Status: PREPARATION / EXPERIMENTAL METHODS. No new material-inference optimization campaign has been launched.** This document is a reviewable public summary of the current scientific-development direction; it is not the detailed private source record. No WUFI project, proprietary sensor data, raw station records, site-specific sensor map, local source paths or private numerical outputs are included.

## Scope and relationship to the existing codebase

The earlier REF/HEMP workflow uses WUFI Pro 6.5, supported by both the separate [Python tools](../python_tools/README.md) and the independent Mathematica branch. This preparation phase targets a second REF/HEMP inference campaign with an extended monitoring interval. It is **not** Valters Ribickis's wall-thickness / construction search, and its methods should not be represented as already implemented in his tools.

This note belongs to the Python infrastructure branch. The two long-lived branch histories must remain separate; do **not** merge the Python branch into the Mathematica branch. See [branching policy](BRANCHING.md).

## Evidence already obtained privately

1. A provenance-labelled research-only WAC candidate was generated by replacing absent original measured hours with a historical-analogue series, without smoothing at seams. It is an exploratory reference, **not** accepted actual-year forcing.
2. WUFI6 native command-line execution and fresh export are demonstrated on isolated REF and HEMP project copies. Repeated single-thread REF ASC outputs matched each other exactly. Differences versus archived baselines remain an OPEN numerical-regression gate; output-field identity and units must be independently reconciled.
3. A source-preserving private climate benchmark masked 48 contiguous observational windows with lengths 6, 24, 72 and 168 hours. It compared linear, PCHIP, local-harmonic and Kalman/RTS residual smoothing, with held-out observations excluded from fitting.
4. A second benchmark used **actual-date, ERA5-Land-labelled hourly data delivered by a secondary public API**, with independent local bias corrections, over the same masked windows. Preliminary temperature reconstruction supports actual-date reanalysis for longer gaps. This does not prove accuracy during the truly missing long interval, and the data are not the official original Copernicus CDS extraction.
5. Jointly unconstrained temperature and vapour-pressure reconstructions occasionally imply RH above saturation; a fully physical WAC is NOT yet admitted. Missing indoor climate forcing is a separate issue.
6. A further held-out pilot assessed joint temperature and logit-RH correction (rather than independent temperature and log-vapour-pressure correction) on the same retrospective masks. The logit formulation kept reconstructed RH strictly between 0 and 100% in that pilot; it is **not** a complete physically accepted climate, nor a result for wind, rain, irradiance or long-gap on-site truth.
7. An additional **controlled-regression comparison** now evaluates ERA-driven ordinary and delay DMDc-style residual models, including a finite nonlinear lifting library. All configured variants completed masked-gap tests, including a small set of 30-/60-day synthetic holes; no consistently superior method emerged against ERA+bounded Kalman across T, RH and vapour pressure. A targeted held-out-data poisoning check confirmed unchanged model predictions in one representative mask. These are ARX/DMDc-style numerical predictors, **not** a completed Koopman eigenmode or robust full-spectrum validation.
8. Original source meteorology has now been independently re-aggregated from the preserved private MongoDB export. This independently reconciles the observed-hour outdoor temperature, humidity and wind-speed aggregation with the earlier WAC provenance and confirms the post-gap indoor monitoring data are not represented by the older padded interior WAC. A source-flagged, **research-only** observed-hour interior WAC preview has been prepared; unobserved hours remain excluded from scientific climate admission.
9. The matched reconstructed hourly predictions have also undergone multiscale and event diagnostics: diurnal amplitude/phase, lagged increments, multi-hour contrast energies, inter-variable correlations and high-RH thresholds. Preliminary results favour retaining ERA-driven bounded Kalman smoothing for humid-event reconstruction over the tested DMDc variants in longer masks. This is NOT yet a full validated PSD/wavelet/POD eigenmode analysis or a production climate.
10. **Direct authenticated Copernicus CDS ERA5-Land acquisition has now succeeded.** The official time-series source covers the monitoring period with eight climate variables; its hourly field groups and source hashes were verified privately, and the official temperature/dewpoint series closely agrees with the prior secondary reanalysis. The CDS time-series is ARCO-derived/experimental and remains a research-only candidate until site geometry, accumulated radiation/rain semantics, solar decomposition and interior boundary uncertainty are accepted.
11. **An initial indoor model benchmarking framework (M0–M5) has now been implemented privately.** Original indoor observation windows are withheld identically across a refitted weather baseline, bounded effective RC dynamics, physical-state/RTS smoothing, a compact controlled DMDc predictor, limited two-pole thermal studies and a weather-exogenous SARIMAX comparator. An auxiliary warm-side wall temperature channel improves some longer-gap reconstructions within known operating regimes, but cross-regime transfer and long-gap uncertainty calibration are not yet acceptable. Separate adversarial holdout-invariance tests passed; these are *research-only* algorithmic benchmarks, not an inferred true climate history.
12. **A further dynamic indoor-state validation gate is completed privately (M6/M7 experimental).** M6 couples indoor air temperature with a lagged near-interior wall-temperature state and a latent effective heating/cooling discrepancy; M7 tests an effective vapour-concentration buffer and latent moisture input. Both were evaluated against the same source-observed, genuinely withheld indoor periods. In-regime auxiliary wall temperatures improve some long-gap reconstructions and predictions of separate withheld wall probes, but do not reliably improve short gaps or vapour-pressure/RH reconstruction. Regime-to-regime transfer is unstable, the effective moisture-buffer contribution is not identifiable from currently admitted observations, and separately tuned process-noise intervals fail long-horizon coverage. No true missing indoor history, operating schedule, room ventilation rate, or material coefficient is recovered or claimed.
13. The benchmark scripts, masks, original official CDS and secondary reanalysis responses, outputs, SHA-256 manifests, versioned methods and optimization handoff are saved in a **private project archive**, not committed here. No private results or data are available in this public source repository.

These are distinct evidence levels: acquisition, source-QC, masked reconstruction benchmark, native WUFI smoke testing, and final multi-case physical validation. A result at one level does not automatically pass the others.

## Climate reconstruction development plan (approved, not completed)

- Primary forcing candidate: actual-date 2026 ERA5-Land with source-specific meteorological-variable provenance, local correction and uncertainty. The official CDS time-series source is now acquired and hourly-audited, and independently matched to a small original-GRIB solar/precipitation accumulation spot check; physical site-specific climate admission is still required before WUFI use. Include site coordinate/exposure, UTC versus fixed-standard-time end labels, altitude, accumulated radiation/rain units, pressure, wind direction and solar-horizontal/direct/diffuse closure.
- Reference: retain uncorrected actual-date reanalysis and the historical-analogue WAC as independent scenarios, not ground truth.
- Short interruptions: benchmark linear, PCHIP, compact boundary tapers and state-space/Kalman smoothers using blocked observed-data masks.
- DMDc: ERA reanalysis as exogenous control; test delay-embedded and low-dimensional nonlinear lifted variants against simpler models, not just in-sample residuals. DMD/HODMD/POD/wavelets/multiscale methods assess modal/diurnal/synoptic structure and out-of-sample consistency; they are not direct estimators of WUFI material constants.
- Physical constraints: jointly parameterize temperature and bounded RH or vapour pressure conditionally on temperature; report source variable, saturation-bound violations, vapour pressure and uncertainty. Do not silently clip model forcing without flagging the original inadmissibility.
- Both interior and exterior boundary records must be reconstructed for observed post-gap periods. The long unknown interior period needs explicit, separately validated scenarios.
- Validation: blocked withheld windows, seasonal stratification, extremes and wetting events, diurnal amplitude/phase, power spectra/wavelets, temporal autocorrelation, variable cross-correlations, realistic uncertainty envelopes, and WUFI6 wall-state sensitivity.

A limited DMDc-style controlled reconstruction comparison is completed as an exploratory numerical benchmark. Original CDS time-series and secondary-provider thermodynamic agreement have been checked, and a bounded Kalman source-swap regression reproduced the earlier results on unchanged masked windows. **Full DMD/HODMD/POD eigenmode, spectral/wavelet and physically admitted WAC validation remain open**, as does extended-horizon native WUFI6 acceptance.

## Additional official climate physics and scenario gates (experimental)

- A small original-format ERA5-Land GRIB accumulation extract was independently de-accumulated and matched to the official time-series radiation and precipitation values over the comparable summer hours. This supports the time-series product's stated de-accumulation convention; the full-year/source-exposure gate is still open.
- A version-pinned `pvlib` solar-geometry and Erbs decomposition audit checked physical nonnegativity, daylight plausibility and DNI/DHI/GHI closure on the official time-series grid. Negative numerical accumulation artefacts are flagged and altered only in separately versioned **derived research data**, never in original source files.
- Official snowfall was added as a modelled precipitation-phase reference. A grid-based liquid-precipitation estimate is **not** treated as actual wind-driven rain at the wall.
- Three exterior and three interior **source-labelled, research-only** climate-scenario series were generated. The original available indoor/outdoor meteorological observations are preserved exactly; missing hours are explicitly classed as conditional reconstructions or assumptions. The unknown indoor operating history is represented by alternative dry, humid and assumed-transition regimes, not a claimed recovered ground truth.
- Independent joint-series checks verified common time axes, measured-hour preservation and preliminary seam criteria. No complete native WUFI WAC or material-property inference is claimed; actual site geometry, time semantics, pressure specification, facade exposure and end-state sensitivities remain to be accepted.

## Approved future WUFI6 material-inference architecture (NOT IMPLEMENTED)

A reusable interface should separate: optimizer proposal -> physically constrained material-curve encoding -> copied WUFI6 project generation -> native WUFI6 execution -> numerical ASC verification -> measurement alignment / objective -> durable trial record.

Parameterization and stages:
1. Screen sensitivities and identifiability before releasing knots.
2. Start with low-dimensional, meaningful material-curve deformations. Enforce sorption storage monotonicity by construction; require physically justified capacity and positivity bounds, but do **not** impose universal monotonic liquid-transport coefficients.
3. Compare FST-PSO, an adaptive differential-evolution method and CMA-ES using equal WUFI evaluation budgets, versions, seeds and failure rules.
4. Perform local refinement and only then selectively release more knots.
5. Revalidate on independent sensor periods, weather branches and initial states, retaining parameter uncertainty and model-form limitations.

The proposed observation targets are scalar uncertainty-aware T/RH and T/vapour-pressure objectives, plus a vapour-pressure-only diagnostic comparator. Derived vapour pressure is not an independent third measurement to add on top of both T and RH. Sorption hysteresis cannot be natively resolved within the present WUFI6 material representation; the resulting effective material function must be labelled accordingly.

The inner optimization loop should avoid figure export, record source/project hashes and all objective components, and use a robust, restartable single-controller execution ledger. Full unattended optimization is **reserved for a separate future campaign**, subject to acceptance gates.
